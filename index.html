    Daily unique players

    Distinct players across each whole UTC day, counted once even if they reconnect or change names. Recorded sessions spanning midnight count on both days. Missing logouts count only on the login day. Totals cover the available activity log and are independent of the filters above.

    Player count & round duration

    Each point represents a round. Includes all recorded rounds with valid counts and durations, including those without timestamps. This plot is independent of the time and session filters above. Points have slight horizontal jitter to reveal overlaps; details show the original values. The orange curve is a quadratic regression fitted to all original rounds (equal weight per round). Beyond the highest recorded count, the dashed purple curve and shaded region extrapolate the same fit to 50 players. Extrapolation is uncertain; negative estimates are model outputs, not valid round durations.
